Ethics

Ethical principles, author responsibility, and platform limits.

Ethical Position

GateBreakOpen requires authors to act responsibly, transparently, and lawfully. Authors remain solely responsible for the accuracy, originality, legality, ethical compliance, references, declarations, images, tables, figures, data, permissions, and consequences of their submitted and published work.

No Primary Human or Animal Research

GateBreakOpen does not accept primary human research, animal research, clinical trials, experimental studies on people or animals, direct patient data collection, or active intervention-based studies. Contributions must remain within the non-interventional scope of the platform.

Prohibited Content

GateBreakOpen may reject, suspend, remove, or preserve for review any content involving unlawful material, racism, discrimination, denigration, hate speech, religious proselytism, pedophilia or child sexual abuse material, terrorism-related content, extremist propaganda, incitement to violence, privacy violations, plagiarism, defamatory content, fabricated or manipulated data, or other serious legal or ethical concerns.

Platform Non-Endorsement

GateBreakOpen does not endorse, certify, validate, verify, recommend, or assume responsibility for the scientific, legal, ethical, medical, technical, or factual content of published works. Publication does not represent approval by GateBreakOpen.

Editorial QA Is Not Peer Review

GateBreakOpen may perform basic editorial and administrative checks, including formatting, file organization, declarations, references, tables, figures, and consistency checks. These checks do not constitute peer review, scientific validation, methodological approval, or endorsement of author claims.
