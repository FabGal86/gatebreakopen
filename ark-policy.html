ARK Policy

GateBreakOpen uses ARK-style persistent identification rather than DOI assignment.

ARK-Based Identification

GateBreakOpen may assign ARK-style identifiers to published articles and related publication objects. ARKs are used to support transparent identification, archival organization, and persistent reference within the platform.

No DOI Assignment

GateBreakOpen does not assign DOI identifiers. The platform uses an independent identification model that remains distinct from DOI-based journal publishing systems.

Landing Pages

Each assigned identifier should resolve, where technically possible, to a landing page or archive record containing title, author information, publication year, article type, license, and available files.

Persistence Commitment

If a file is corrected, suspended, replaced, or removed, GateBreakOpen may maintain an archive notice or landing page record when possible.
