Call For Papers

GateBreak accepts contributions for online publication on a continuous basis. The platform publishes exclusively online and follows a continuous publication model, without closed editorial windows, fixed submission periods, or restricted publication cycles.

This means that authors are not required to wait for a specific call, issue, deadline, or editorial season in order to submit their work. GateBreak does not operate through limited publication slots or temporary submission windows. Instead, it remains open to receiving contributions throughout the year.

GateBreak welcomes works from students, professionals, researchers, independent authors, and affiliated authors who wish to make their original work visible in an accessible online format. Contributions may include research papers, professional analyses, essays, applied projects, technical documents, educational materials, reflective articles, case discussions, methodological notes, or other forms of structured intellectual and professional production.

The publication process is designed to be simple, direct, and author-centered. Once a work is received, GateBreak prepares the material, performs a structural checklist, organizes it according to its editorial format, and publishes it online. The purpose of this process is to support readability, structure, accessibility, and presentation, without imposing unnecessary content modifications or editorial reinterpretations.

GateBreak does not require authors to adapt their work to internal citation strategies, does not impose the use of internal articles, and does not request contextual integrations that are not part of the author’s original project. The author remains responsible for the content, the method, the ethical position, the sources, and the intellectual direction of the work.

Publication on GateBreak does not represent scientific validation, peer-review approval, endorsement, certification, or institutional recognition by the platform. GateBreak offers visibility, editorial preparation, structural organization, and online publication, while preserving the author’s responsibility and the original identity of the work.

GateBreak is completely free of APCs. Authors are not charged article processing fees or publication fees. GateBreak recognizes that producing a paper or professional contribution already requires time, knowledge, effort, and dedication. The platform therefore does not place an additional economic barrier between authors and the possibility of making their work publicly available.
