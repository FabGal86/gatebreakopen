Useful Links

This page lists selected resources that are useful for students, independent authors, academics, and researchers. The focus is on free access, open infrastructure, non-commercial or community-led scholarly communication, transparent metadata, open citations, repositories, author identifiers, and free research tools.

Selection rule: this list avoids paywalled, APC-driven, citation-manipulative, closed, or strongly freemium tools. Some services may offer optional paid institutional or premium services, but the links below are included because their core scholarly value is free to access or aligned with open, public, non-commercial, and anti-gatekeeping knowledge practices.
