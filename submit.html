Submission

To submit an article to GateBreakOpen, send your manuscript and the required signed forms by email. GateBreakOpen accepts secondary research, literature-based articles, conceptual papers, essays, commentaries, methodological papers, and other non-interventional scholarly contributions.

GateBreakOpen currently publishes only English-language content. Authors are therefore required to submit manuscripts in English, or to provide an English version before publication can be considered.

Submission

What to Send by Email

Send the following material to submission@gatebreakopen.org: the article in Word or PDF format, the signed Complete Publication, Privacy, and Full Policy Acceptance Form, and any tables, figures, appendices, or supplementary files where applicable.

Submission

Publication Identifier: ARK, not DOI

GateBreakOpen assigns an ARK identifier to eligible publications. GateBreakOpen does not assign a DOI. This choice supports GateBreakOpen's independent, zero-cost, flexible, multi-format, online-only, and not peer-reviewed model.

A GateBreakOpen identifier may follow a chronological structure, for example: ark:/NAAN/gatebreakopen.article.2026.0001. The final structure may be adapted according to the official ARK/NAAN configuration used by GateBreakOpen.

Submission

Mandatory Information to Include

The submission must include: title, subtitle, author name or author names, declaration of affiliation or independent-author status, ORCID (mandatory), last academic qualification (mandatory), certifying institution, university, institute, or awarding body (mandatory), abstract of maximum 300 words, area, keywords with a maximum of 6 keywords, and objective of the article with a maximum of 200 words.

Data Availability Statement (Mandatory)

Every submission must include a Data Availability Statement. The author must clearly declare whether the work uses public data, published data, secondary sources, aggregate datasets, tables, figures, supplementary material, unpublished evidence, or no underlying dataset. The statement is mandatory even when no dataset exists or when data cannot be shared.

Authors must select one of the following options and include it in the manuscript:

  • Publicly available data: the data supporting this work are publicly available in an open repository or lawful public source at the following link: [insert link].
  • Data available on request: the data are not publicly deposited but may be requested from the author, where lawful and appropriate, through the author’s ORCID profile, email address, or declared contact information.
  • Data included in the article: all data generated, analyzed, summarized, or discussed in this work are included within the article, tables, figures, appendices, supplementary materials, or cited public sources.
  • Data not shareable due to restrictions: the data cannot be publicly shared because of legal, privacy, confidentiality, contractual, ethical, institutional, copyright, third-party, security, or other legitimate restrictions. The author must briefly explain the reason.
  • Not applicable: this work does not use datasets, raw data, statistical analyses, empirical materials, unpublished evidence, or data-based claims.

GateBreakOpen does not verify, validate, certify, audit, or guarantee the declared data. The purpose of this requirement is transparency: readers must be able to see whether the work is supported by public, requestable, included, restricted, or non-applicable data.

Submission

References

References must be listed at the end of the paper and must also be cited within the text. The author is responsible for ensuring that all references are real, accurate, relevant, correctly written, and consistent with the claims made in the article.

GateBreakOpen will manage the conversion of the reference list into the required standard format. However, the author must provide a complete reference list and must ensure that all references are already cited in the text before submission. GateBreakOpen may correct or standardize the format of references, but it does not replace the author's responsibility for the existence, accuracy, relevance, correctness, and proper in-text use of each source.

Submission

Tables and Figures

Tables and figures must be submitted as separate files from the main text file. GateBreakOpen will insert tables and figures in numerical order before the References section, according to the numbering and indications provided by the author.

The author must number each table and figure clearly, must indicate in the text where each table or figure should be placed, and must provide a short description explaining what each table or figure represents. The author remains fully responsible for the accuracy, originality, legality, copyright compliance, and correct interpretation of all tables, figures, images, graphs, diagrams, screenshots, and visual materials submitted to GateBreakOpen.

Not Accepted

GateBreakOpen does not accept primary research involving direct testing, experimentation, intervention, observation, or new data collection on humans or animals. This includes clinical trials, animal testing, experiments, interviews, surveys collecting new participant data, identifiable case reports, or studies involving sensitive personal, clinical, or confidential data.

GateBreakOpen does not accept content involving racism, denigration, discrimination, hate speech, terrorism, incitement to violence, extremist propaganda, unlawful conduct, harassment, defamation, intimidation, violent radicalization, or proselytism. Religious, ideological, political, or social topics may be considered only when treated in a lawful, non-proselytizing, non-inciting, non-denigrating, scholarly, critical, or educational manner.

GateBreakOpen is totally independent and free for authors. It applies a strict separation between three principles: no author fees, no manipulation, and no favoritism.

  • No Manipulation: GateBreakOpen does not impose forced references, citation manipulation, artificial visibility, or content changes designed to manipulate the author's work or benefit third parties.
  • No Favoritism: GateBreakOpen does not provide preferential treatment to authors, institutions, sponsors, funders, organizations, companies, ideologies, or external interests.

GateBreakOpen exists exclusively as a digital and online platform. It does not publish, distribute, sell, archive, or circulate printed issues, printed volumes, printed journals, printed books, or any other physical publication format. All materials published through GateBreakOpen are made available only in digital form, through online pages, downloadable files, electronic archives, and related web-based resources. GateBreakOpen is not a peer-reviewed journal and does not perform scientific validation, academic certification, methodological approval, or endorsement of published content. Articles published on GateBreakOpen are not subject to formal anonymous peer review, evaluation by independent external reviewers, or scientific verification by an editorial board, unless this is expressly stated for specific initiatives, collections, or sections.
