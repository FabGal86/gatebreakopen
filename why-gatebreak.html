Why GateBreakOpen?

The rationale behind GateBreakOpen and its independent publishing model.

Why GateBreakOpen Exists

GateBreakOpen exists to provide a low-barrier, author-responsible, independent publishing space for intellectual work that does not require article processing charges, editorial favoritism, citation manipulation, or forced gatekeeping.

Author Independence

The platform does not reshape author ideas to fit institutional, commercial, ideological, or citation-based interests. Authors remain responsible for their own interpretations, conclusions, and claims.

Zero-Cost Model

GateBreakOpen is designed to avoid economic barriers for authors while maintaining clear policy boundaries and transparent publication rules.

Platform Limits

GateBreakOpen does not perform peer review and does not validate, certify, or endorse the scientific, medical, legal, ethical, or factual content of published works.
