What Can Be Reported

Reports and Removal Requests

How to Submit a Report

Send your report to submission@gatebreakopen.org with the subject line: GateBreakOpen Report / Removal Request.

Reports and Removal Requests

Information to Include

Include the article title, article link if available, a clear explanation of the issue, your contact email, evidence or documentation supporting the report, and whether you request correction, clarification, temporary suspension, or removal.

Reports and Removal Requests

GateBreakOpen Response

GateBreakOpen may review the report, contact the author, request clarification, temporarily suspend access, add a notice, publish a correction, or remove the article where appropriate. GateBreakOpen reserves the right to reject abusive, unfounded, incomplete, or bad-faith reports.
