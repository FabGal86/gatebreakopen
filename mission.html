Mission

The mission and identity of GateBreakOpen.

Mission

GateBreakOpen exists to provide an independent, zero-cost, online-only space for scholarly, educational, theoretical, conceptual, methodological, reflective, and literature-based works. Its mission is to reduce access barriers, support intellectual independence, and make author-led publication possible without article processing charges, editorial favoritism, citation manipulation, or forced academic gatekeeping.

Free Minds, Open Pages

The platform is built around the idea that authors should be able to share structured intellectual work while remaining fully responsible for their content, interpretations, references, declarations, and conclusions. GateBreakOpen does not reshape author ideas to fit institutional, commercial, ideological, or citation-based interests.

Scope of the Mission

GateBreakOpen supports non-interventional publication formats, including reviews, theoretical articles, conceptual papers, educational articles, methodological papers, essays, open letters, monographs, book chapters, professional reflections, and other secondary or scholarly contributions. GateBreakOpen does not accept primary human or animal research.

Independence

GateBreakOpen is independent, non-peer-reviewed, AI-assisted, zero-cost, and online-only. Publication on GateBreakOpen does not imply scientific validation, academic certification, medical validation, institutional endorsement, legal approval, or ethical approval by the platform.
