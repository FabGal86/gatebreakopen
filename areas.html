Areas

GateBreak is open to multiple areas of secondary, conceptual, educational, methodological, theoretical, and non-interventional scholarly work. The areas below indicate the main fields accepted by the platform. All submissions must respect the GateBreak Policy, must not involve prohibited primary human or animal research, and must not contain unlawful, discriminatory, violent, extremist, or proselytizing content.

Health, Medicine, and Healthcare

Secondary research, reviews, commentary, education, methodology, policy analysis, ethics, healthcare systems, public health, clinical literature, and evidence synthesis without primary human or animal research.

Nursing and Allied Health Professions

Literature-based work on nursing practice, rehabilitation, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, professional education, care models, and healthcare teamwork.

Psychology and Behavioral Sciences

Reviews, theory, methods, ethics, education, and conceptual work on behavior, cognition, mental processes, assessment, and psychological literature without collecting new participant data.

Education and Pedagogy

Articles on teaching, learning, curriculum, assessment, educational methods, digital education, training, and educational theory using existing literature or non-interventional approaches.

Social Sciences

Secondary and conceptual work on society, institutions, culture, organizations, inequalities, communication, social behavior, and public issues.

Sociology and Anthropology

Literature-based, theoretical, historical, and critical work on social structures, communities, cultures, practices, identity, and human organization.

Economics, Management, and Organization

Reviews and conceptual papers on management, leadership, work organization, decision-making, business models, economic behavior, labor, innovation, and organizational processes.

Law, Policy, and Governance

Analyses of legal frameworks, public policy, governance, regulation, rights, institutions, compliance, and ethical or social implications of rules and decisions.

Ethics and Bioethics

Conceptual, philosophical, literature-based, and policy work on ethical issues, responsibility, consent, autonomy, justice, integrity, research ethics, and professional conduct.

Philosophy

Theoretical and analytical articles on knowledge, ethics, logic, meaning, science, mind, society, values, and philosophical traditions.

History and Historical Studies

Historical analyses of events, movements, institutions, ideas, authors, documents, professional fields, or scientific and cultural developments.

Communication, Media, and Journalism

Articles on media systems, digital communication, public discourse, misinformation, publishing, writing, journalism, and communication ethics.

Information Science and Libraries

Reviews and analyses on information organization, documentation, bibliometrics, repositories, archives, scholarly communication, and knowledge access.

Data Science and Statistics

Conceptual, methodological, educational, and secondary work on data analysis, statistical methods, visualization, modeling, evidence synthesis, and interpretation.

Artificial Intelligence and Digital Technologies

Reviews, educational articles, methods, ethics, policy analysis, and conceptual work on AI, chatbots, automation, software, digital tools, and human-computer interaction.

Computer Science and Software Methods

Methodological and technical notes on software, programming workflows, algorithms, computational methods, applications, and digital systems without harmful or unlawful content.

Engineering and Applied Technologies

Literature-based and conceptual work on engineering methods, systems, design, technology applications, sustainability, risk, and innovation.

Environmental Studies and Sustainability

Reviews and analyses on climate, environment, sustainability, conservation, resources, public policy, ecological issues, and environmental ethics using existing sources.

Agriculture and Food Systems

Secondary research and policy or technical analysis on food systems, agriculture, production, sustainability, safety, and innovation without prohibited animal experimentation.

Architecture, Design, and Urban Studies

Conceptual, historical, social, technical, and policy work on buildings, cities, design, planning, space, infrastructure, and urban life.

Arts, Humanities, and Cultural Studies

Essays, reviews, criticism, and conceptual work on literature, arts, culture, aesthetics, heritage, language, media, and human expression.

Linguistics and Language Studies

Theoretical, educational, historical, and literature-based work on language, communication, translation, discourse, terminology, and writing.

Religious Studies

Critical, historical, sociological, philosophical, or literature-based analysis of religion and belief systems, allowed only when non-proselytizing, non-inciting, lawful, scholarly, and non-denigrating.

Political Science and International Relations

Analyses of institutions, governance, policy, political theory, international systems, conflict, diplomacy, public decision-making, and social movements using lawful scholarly approaches.

Security, Risk, and Safety Studies

Secondary and policy work on safety, risk, preparedness, governance, prevention, resilience, and security topics, excluding propaganda, operational harm, extremism, or unlawful instruction.

Open Science and Scholarly Publishing

Articles on publishing models, editorial practices, research communication, academic evaluation, repositories, peer review, dissemination, and access to knowledge.

Interdisciplinary Studies

Work combining multiple fields, methods, theories, or perspectives, provided that the article remains secondary, non-interventional, lawful, and compliant with GateBreak policy.

Life Sciences and Environment

Literature-based, theoretical, methodological, educational, and non-interventional contributions related to biological sciences, environmental studies, ecology, sustainability, ecosystems, and life systems. GateBreak does not accept primary human or animal experimentation.

Marketing and Digital Marketing

Contributions related to marketing theory, consumer behavior, branding, communication strategies, digital marketing, online platforms, social media communication, and analytical or educational perspectives.

Business, Economics, and Finance

Contributions related to business studies, management, economics, financial systems, markets, accounting, entrepreneurship, organizational analysis, and economic or financial theory.

Public Health and Health Promotion

Non-interventional and literature-based contributions related to public health, prevention, health promotion, community health, healthcare education, and policy-oriented health analysis.

Digital Health and Health Technologies

Contributions related to digital health, health technologies, telemedicine, health informatics, AI in healthcare, digital tools, and educational or methodological perspectives.

Biotechnology and Biomedical Sciences

Theoretical, conceptual, educational, methodological, review-based, or secondary contributions related to biotechnology, biomedical sciences, and biomedical innovation, without primary laboratory or human/animal experimentation.

Pharmaceutical Sciences

Contributions related to pharmaceutical sciences, drug policy, medication education, pharmacological literature reviews, methodology, and secondary or conceptual analyses.

Veterinary and Animal Studies

Literature-based, educational, theoretical, or policy-oriented contributions related to veterinary topics and animal studies, without primary animal experimentation or direct animal research.

Sports Science and Physical Activity

Contributions related to sports science, exercise, physical activity, training theory, health promotion, education, performance concepts, and non-interventional or literature-based analysis.

Nutrition and Food Sciences

Contributions related to nutrition, food sciences, dietary education, food systems, public health nutrition, and literature-based or conceptual analysis.

Tourism, Hospitality, and Cultural Heritage

Contributions related to tourism, hospitality, cultural heritage, destination studies, travel systems, cultural promotion, and educational or policy-oriented analysis.

Entrepreneurship and Innovation

Contributions related to entrepreneurship, innovation, start-ups, business models, creative enterprise, organizational innovation, and conceptual or methodological frameworks.

Accounting and Auditing

Contributions related to accounting, auditing, reporting, financial documentation, governance, professional standards, and educational or analytical perspectives.

Human Resources and Work Psychology

Contributions related to human resources, organizational behavior, work psychology, leadership, workplace wellbeing, training, professional development, and management education.

Logistics, Operations, and Supply Chain

Contributions related to logistics, operations management, supply chains, production systems, service processes, organizational flows, and methodological or educational models.

Public Administration

Contributions related to public administration, public management, governance, institutional processes, public services, administrative reform, and policy implementation.

International Development and Cooperation

Contributions related to international development, cooperation, global challenges, humanitarian systems, development policy, sustainability, and interdisciplinary analysis.

Migration, Diversity, and Gender Studies

Contributions related to migration, diversity, inclusion, gender studies, social equity, cultural analysis, and scholarly non-discriminatory perspectives.

Criminology, Security, and Forensic Studies

Contributions related to criminology, security, risk, forensic studies, safety systems, crime prevention, legal-social analysis, and educational or review-based work.

Geography, Territory, and Urban Systems

Contributions related to geography, territorial studies, spatial systems, urban analysis, regional development, mobility, land use, and socio-environmental planning.

Energy, Climate, and Sustainability Studies

Contributions related to energy systems, climate change, sustainability, environmental policy, resilience, ecological transition, and interdisciplinary analysis.

Emergency, Disaster Risk, and Resilience Studies

Contributions related to emergency management, disaster risk reduction, resilience, safety planning, crisis systems, preparedness, and recovery frameworks.

Scope Note for All Areas

All areas are interpreted within GateBreak's non-interventional scope. GateBreak accepts literature-based, theoretical, conceptual, methodological, educational, reflective, policy-oriented, and secondary-data contributions. GateBreak does not accept primary human or animal research.
